*temp boss_hp 200
*temp phase 1
*temp fight_round 0
*temp boss_attack 20
*temp enraged false

The iron door groans open. A massive stone golem stirs to life.

"INTRUDER. YOU SHALL NOT PASS."

*label boss_fight
*set fight_round + 1

*if boss_hp <= 100
  *if phase = 1
    *set phase 2
    *set boss_attack + 10
    *set enraged true
    The golem's eyes flare red! It enters a berserk rage!

Round ${fight_round}. @{enraged ENRAGED |} Boss HP: ${boss_hp}. Your HP: ${hp}.

*if boss_hp <= 0
  *goto boss_victory
*if hp <= 0
  *goto boss_defeat

*choice
  #Heavy attack
    *temp damage 0
    *rand damage (attack) (attack + 10)
    *set boss_hp - damage
    You smash the golem for ${damage} damage!
    *temp hit 0
    *rand hit boss_attack (boss_attack + 15)
    *temp mitigated (hit - defense)
    *if mitigated < 0
      *set mitigated 0
    *set hp - mitigated
    The golem retaliates! ${mitigated} damage!
    *goto boss_fight
  #Aim for the cracks
    *temp precision_roll 0
    *rand precision_roll 1 10
    *if precision_roll > 5
      *temp damage 0
      *rand damage (attack + 5) (attack + 20)
      *set boss_hp - damage
      You find a weak point! ${damage} critical damage!
    *else
      Your attack glances off solid stone.
    *temp hit 0
    *rand hit boss_attack (boss_attack + 10)
    *temp mitigated (hit - defense)
    *if mitigated < 0
      *set mitigated 0
    *set hp - mitigated
    The golem swings! ${mitigated} damage!
    *goto boss_fight
  *selectable_if (potions > 0) #Heal with a potion
    *set potions - 1
    *temp heal 0
    *rand heal 30 50
    *set hp + heal
    *if hp > max_hp
      *set hp max_hp
    You drink a potion. HP: ${hp}/${max_hp}.
    *temp hit 0
    *rand hit (boss_attack - 5) boss_attack
    *temp mitigated (hit - defense)
    *if mitigated < 0
      *set mitigated 0
    *set hp - mitigated
    The golem strikes while you heal! ${mitigated} damage!
    *goto boss_fight
  *selectable_if (enraged) #Wait for an opening
    *temp dodge_roll 0
    *rand dodge_roll 1 10
    *if dodge_roll > 3
      The berserk golem overextends!
      *temp damage 0
      *rand damage (attack + 10) (attack + 25)
      *set boss_hp - damage
      A devastating counter for ${damage}!
    *else
      You mistime it! The golem crushes you.
      *temp hit 0
      *rand hit (boss_attack + 5) (boss_attack + 20)
      *set hp - hit
      ${hit} unmitigated damage!
    *goto boss_fight

*label boss_victory
*set boss_defeated true
*set treasure_collected + 10

The golem crumbles into rubble. Behind it, a hoard of ancient treasure.

Victory in ${fight_round} rounds!

*choice
  #Claim the treasure and leave
    You gather armfuls of gold and gems.
    *goto boss_exit
  #Search for something special
    *set attack + 5
    *set defense + 5
    Among the rubble you find an enchanted ring. Its power flows into you.
    *goto boss_exit

*label boss_defeat
The golem's fist comes down one final time.

You lasted ${fight_round} rounds against the dungeon boss. A valiant effort.
*ending "Fallen to the Guardian"

*label boss_exit
You emerge from the dungeon, victorious.

Final stats: ${encounters} encounters, ${rooms_cleared} rooms explored, ${treasure_collected} treasure collected.

The Irondelve Dungeon is conquered!
*finish
