*temp current_room ""
*temp encounter_roll 0
*temp enemy_hp 0
*temp loot_roll 0

*label dungeon_hub

HP: ${hp}/${max_hp}. Potions: ${potions}. Keys: ${keys}. Rooms cleared: ${rooms_cleared}.

*if rooms_cleared >= 6
  A massive iron door looms before you. The boss chamber.
  *if keys >= 2
    You have enough keys to unlock it.
    *choice
      #Enter the boss chamber
        *goto_scene boss
      #Rest and prepare first
        *gosub rest
        *goto dungeon_hub
  *else
    The door requires 2 keys. You have ${keys}. Explore more rooms.

The corridor branches in several directions.

*choice
  *hide_reuse #Take the left passage (dark and narrow)
    *set current_room "narrow"
    *gosub enter_room
    *goto dungeon_hub
  *hide_reuse #Take the right passage (wide and echoing)
    *set current_room "wide"
    *gosub enter_room
    *goto dungeon_hub
  *hide_reuse #Descend a crumbling staircase
    *set current_room "stairs"
    *gosub enter_room
    *goto dungeon_hub
  *hide_reuse #Crawl through a gap in the wall
    *set current_room "gap"
    *gosub enter_room
    *goto dungeon_hub
  *hide_reuse #Follow the sound of running water
    *set current_room "water"
    *gosub enter_room
    *goto dungeon_hub
  *hide_reuse #Push open a rusted gate
    *set current_room "gate"
    *gosub enter_room
    *goto dungeon_hub
  *selectable_if (potions > 0) #Use a potion
    *gosub use_potion
    *goto dungeon_hub
  #Retreat to the surface
    *goto retreat

*label enter_room
*set rooms_cleared + 1
*rand encounter_roll 1 10

*if encounter_roll <= 6
  *gosub combat_encounter
*elseif encounter_roll <= 8
  *gosub treasure_room
*else
  *gosub key_room

*return

*label combat_encounter
*set encounters + 1
*set enemy_hp (30 + (encounters * 5))

A creature lunges from the shadows!

*label combat_round

Enemy HP: ${enemy_hp}. Your HP: ${hp}.

*if enemy_hp <= 0
  *goto combat_victory
*if hp <= 0
  *goto death

*choice
  #Strike!
    *temp damage 0
    *rand damage (attack - 5) (attack + 5)
    *set enemy_hp - damage
    You deal ${damage} damage!
    *temp enemy_damage 0
    *rand enemy_damage 5 (10 + (encounters * 2))
    *temp mitigated 0
    *set mitigated (enemy_damage - defense)
    *if mitigated < 0
      *set mitigated 0
    *set hp - mitigated
    The creature strikes back! ${mitigated} damage after your armor absorbs the rest.
    *goto combat_round
  #Dodge and counter
    *temp dodge_roll 0
    *rand dodge_roll 1 10
    *if dodge_roll > 4
      You dodge the attack!
      *temp damage 0
      *rand damage (attack - 3) (attack + 3)
      *set enemy_hp - damage
      Counter-attack for ${damage}!
    *else
      You stumble! The creature rakes you.
      *temp enemy_damage 0
      *rand enemy_damage 8 (15 + (encounters * 2))
      *set hp - enemy_damage
      ${enemy_damage} damage taken!
    *goto combat_round
  *selectable_if (potions > 0) #Use a potion mid-fight
    *gosub use_potion
    *temp enemy_damage 0
    *rand enemy_damage 3 8
    *set hp - enemy_damage
    The creature strikes while you drink! ${enemy_damage} damage.
    *goto combat_round

*label combat_victory
*rand loot_roll 1 10
*if loot_roll > 7
  You find a potion on the corpse!
  *set potions + 1
*if loot_roll <= 3
  A few coins glint among the remains.
  *set treasure_collected + 1
Victory! The creature falls.
*return

*label treasure_room
*set treasure_collected + 1

A glittering cache of treasure!

*choice
  #Take the gold
    *set treasure_collected + 2
    You stuff your pockets with coins.
    *return
  #Search for hidden items
    *rand loot_roll 1 10
    *if loot_roll > 5
      A healing potion hidden behind the gold!
      *set potions + 1
      *set treasure_collected + 1
    *else
      Nothing else of value.
    *return

*label key_room
An ornate chest sits on a pedestal. Inside, a heavy iron key.

*set keys + 1

You now have ${keys} key(s).
*return

*label use_potion
*set potions - 1
*temp heal 0
*rand heal 25 40
*set hp + heal
*if hp > max_hp
  *set hp max_hp
You drink a potion and recover ${heal} HP. HP: ${hp}/${max_hp}.
*return

*label rest
You find a quiet alcove and rest briefly.
*temp heal 0
*rand heal 10 20
*set hp + heal
*if hp > max_hp
  *set hp max_hp
Recovered ${heal} HP. HP: ${hp}/${max_hp}.
*return

*label death
Your torch sputters out. The dungeon claims another adventurer.

Rooms cleared: ${rooms_cleared}. Encounters survived: ${encounters}.
*ending "Fallen in the Deep"

*label retreat
You climb back to the surface, blinking in the sunlight.

*if rooms_cleared >= 3
  A respectable delve. You'll return stronger.
*else
  Perhaps you weren't ready for the dungeon.

Rooms explored: ${rooms_cleared}. Treasure collected: ${treasure_collected}.
*finish
