*temp areas_searched 0
*temp found_weapon_here false
*temp found_documents_here false
*temp found_glass_here false

You arrive at Blackwood Manor's study. The chalk outline is still on the floor.

*label scene_hub

*if areas_searched >= 4
  You've thoroughly searched the area. Nothing more to find.
  *goto scene_exit

Areas searched: ${areas_searched} of 4.

*choice
  *hide_reuse #Search the desk
    *set areas_searched + 1
    You rifle through the drawers.
    *if areas_searched = 1
      You find insurance documents worth a fortune. Motive!
      *set has_motive true
      *set clues_found + 1
      *set notes & " Motive found."
    *else
      Nothing else of interest in the desk.
    *goto scene_hub
  *hide_reuse #Examine the bookshelf
    *set areas_searched + 1
    A book is out of place. Behind it, a hidden compartment.
    *set clues_found + 1
    *set notes & " Hidden compartment found."
    *goto scene_hub
  *hide_reuse #Check near the window
    *set areas_searched + 1
    Broken glass on the inside. Staged break-in?
    *set found_glass_here true
    *set clues_found + 1
    *set notes & " Staged break-in evidence."
    *goto scene_hub
  *hide_reuse #Look under the furniture
    *set areas_searched + 1
    *set found_weapon_here true
    A letter opener with stains. The murder weapon!
    *set has_weapon true
    *set clues_found + 1
    *set notes & " Weapon found."
    *goto scene_hub
  #Return to case hub
    *goto scene_exit

*label scene_exit
You step back from the crime scene.
*goto_scene startup case_hub
