*temp current_suspect ""
*temp question_count 0
*temp caught_contradiction false

The suspects wait in separate rooms at the station.

*label suspect_select

Who do you want to interview?

*choice
  *hide_reuse #The butler, Mr. Graves
    *set current_suspect "Graves"
    *set question_count 0
    *set caught_contradiction false
    *gosub interview_loop
    *goto suspect_select
  *hide_reuse #The spouse, Lady Blackwood
    *set current_suspect "Blackwood"
    *set question_count 0
    *set caught_contradiction false
    *gosub interview_loop
    *goto suspect_select
  *hide_reuse #The business partner, Mr. Cole
    *set current_suspect "Cole"
    *set question_count 0
    *set caught_contradiction false
    *gosub interview_loop
    *goto suspect_select
  #Return to case hub
    *goto interrogation_exit

*label interview_loop
*set question_count + 1

You sit across from @{(current_suspect = "Graves") Mr. Graves|@{(current_suspect = "Blackwood") Lady Blackwood|Mr. Cole}}.

*if question_count > 4
  The suspect's lawyer intervenes. "My client has answered enough questions."
  *set suspects_interviewed + 1
  *return

Question ${question_count} of 4.

*choice
  #"Where were you that night?"
    *if current_suspect = "Blackwood"
      "I was at the opera. I have my ticket stub."
      *if has_weapon
        But the timeline doesn't add up with the forensic evidence.
        *set caught_contradiction true
        *set has_alibi_broken true
        *set clues_found + 1
    *elseif current_suspect = "Graves"
      "Polishing the silver in the pantry, as I do every evening."
    *else
      "Working late at the office. My secretary can confirm."
    *goto interview_loop
  #"Did you have any grievances with the victim?"
    *if current_suspect = "Cole"
      "We had our disagreements, but nothing beyond business."
      His eye twitches. He's hiding something.
      *set clues_found + 1
    *elseif current_suspect = "Blackwood"
      "We were happily married." She dabs her eyes with a handkerchief.
    *else
      "Lord Blackwood was a fair employer. I had no complaints."
    *goto interview_loop
  #"Do you know anyone who would want the victim dead?"
    *if current_suspect = "Graves"
      "I... perhaps shouldn't say. But Lady Blackwood and Lord Blackwood argued frequently about money."
      *set clues_found + 1
    *else
      "I can't imagine anyone wanting to harm Lord Blackwood."
    *goto interview_loop
  #End the interview early
    *set suspects_interviewed + 1
    "That will be all for now."
    *return

*label interrogation_exit
You leave the interview rooms.
*goto_scene startup case_hub
