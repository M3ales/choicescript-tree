*create clues_found 0
*create suspects_interviewed 0
*create confidence 0
*create has_weapon false
*create has_motive false
*create has_alibi_broken false
*create detective_rank "Junior"
*create case_closed false
*create notes ""

You are Detective ${detective_rank}, assigned to the Blackwood Manor case.

A body was found in the study. Three suspects. No witnesses.

*label case_hub

Case progress: ${clues_found} clues found. ${suspects_interviewed} suspects interviewed.

*if (clues_found >= 3) and (suspects_interviewed >= 2)
  *set confidence + 20
  You feel ready to make an accusation.

*choice
  #Investigate the crime scene
    *goto_scene crime_scene
  #Interview suspects
    *goto_scene interrogation
  *selectable_if (confidence >= 50) #Make an accusation
    *goto accusation
  #Review your notes
    *gosub review_notes
    *goto case_hub
  *selectable_if (clues_found >= 5) #Request forensic analysis
    *gosub forensics
    *goto case_hub

*label accusation
You gather the suspects in the drawing room.

"I have solved this case."

*choice
  #Accuse the butler
    *if (has_weapon) and (has_motive)
      *goto correct_accusation
    *else
      *goto wrong_accusation
  #Accuse the spouse
    *if (has_alibi_broken) and (has_motive)
      *goto correct_accusation
    *else
      *goto wrong_accusation
  #Accuse the business partner
    *goto wrong_accusation

*label correct_accusation
*set case_closed true
*set detective_rank "Senior"

"The evidence speaks clearly," you declare, laying out each clue.

The guilty party breaks down. Case closed.

Congratulations, Detective. You've been promoted to ${detective_rank}.
*finish

*label wrong_accusation

The accused produces an airtight defense. Your evidence crumbles.

*set confidence - 30

*choice
  #Return to investigating
    Embarrassed, you resolve to find more evidence.
    *goto case_hub
  #Give up the case
    The case goes cold. A failure on your record.
    *ending "Case Unsolved"

*label review_notes
Your case file reads:

*if has_weapon
  - Murder weapon identified: letter opener from the study.
*if has_motive
  - Financial motive confirmed via insurance documents.
*if has_alibi_broken
  - Spouse's alibi has inconsistencies.
*if clues_found = 0
  - No evidence collected yet.

*return

*label forensics
The lab results come back.

*if has_weapon
  Fingerprints on the weapon match one of the suspects.
  *set confidence + 15
*else
  No weapon submitted for analysis.

*return
