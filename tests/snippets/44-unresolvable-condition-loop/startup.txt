*comment --- Loop A: counter behind gosub ---

*label counter_loop
*set attempts + 1

*gosub update_progress

*if (progress < 100)
  *goto counter_loop

*comment --- Loop B: dispatch hub ---

*label dispatch
*if (mood = "happy")
  *goto happy_path
*if (mood = "sad")
  *goto sad_path
*goto dispatch

*label happy_path
You feel great.

*comment --- Loop C: depth-3 nested gosub chain ---
*comment outer: loops on depth, incremented in middle gosub
*comment middle: loops on inner_count, incremented in inner gosub
*comment inner: loops on phase, incremented in deep gosub

*set depth 0
*set inner_count 0
*set phase 0

*label outer_loop
*gosub middle_loop
*if (depth < 6)
  *goto outer_loop

*comment --- Loop D: depth-3 direct loops with gosub mutations ---
*comment wave_loop: exits on wave >= 3, wave incremented in gosub
*comment shield_loop: exits on shield <= 0, shield decremented in gosub
*comment combo_loop: exits on combo >= 5, combo incremented in gosub

*set wave 0
*set shield 100
*set combo 0

*label wave_loop
*gosub do_wave
*if (wave < 3)
  *goto wave_loop

All waves complete.
*finish

*label sad_path
You feel down.
*finish

  *goto middle_spin

*gosub do_shield_tick

  *goto shield_loop
*gosub advance_wave
*return
