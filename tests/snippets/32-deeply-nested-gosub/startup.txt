*create depth 0

*return

  *gosub layer3

*return

*label layer3
*set result (result & "L3>")
*set depth + 1
*gosub layer4
*set result (result & "<L3")
*return

*set depth + 1
*if (depth >= 4)
  *gosub layer5
*set result (result & "<L4")
*return

*set depth + 1
