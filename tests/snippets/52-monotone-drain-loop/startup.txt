*create gold 50

*label shop
Gold: ${gold}.
*choice
  *selectable_if (gold >= 20) #Buy armor (20g).
    *set gold - 20
    You bought armor.
    *goto shop
  *selectable_if (gold >= 10) #Buy potion (10g).
    *set gold - 10
    You bought a potion.
    *goto shop
  #Leave.
    You leave.
    *finish
