*temp drink_count 0
*temp heard_rumor false

The tavern is warm and bustling. A bard plays in the corner.

*label tavern_hub

*choice
  *selectable_if (gold >= 5) #Buy a drink (5 gold)
    *set gold - 5
    *set drink_count + 1
    *set reputation %+ 5
    You enjoy a cold ale.
    *if drink_count >= 3
      The barkeep leans in. "You seem trustworthy. There's an arena champion looking for a real fight."
      *set heard_rumor true
    *goto tavern_hub
  #Talk to the bard
    *gosub bard_conversation
    *goto tavern_hub
  *selectable_if (charm >= 50) #Charm the barmaid for information
    She whispers, "The shopkeeper has a special sword. Pricey, but worth it."
    *set reputation %+ 10
    *goto tavern_hub
  #Leave the tavern
    *goto tavern_exit

*label tavern_exit
You step back out into the village square.
*goto_scene startup village_hub

*label bard_conversation
The bard pauses between songs.

*choice
  #Request a heroic ballad
    *set reputation %+ 5
    The bard sings of ancient heroes. The crowd raises their mugs.
    *return
  #Ask about local news
    "Strange things happening near the old dungeon," the bard says quietly.
    *return
  #Toss a coin (1 gold)
    *if gold >= 1
      *set gold - 1
      *set reputation %+ 10
      The bard nods gratefully and plays your favorite tune.
    *else
      You reach for your purse but find it empty. Embarrassing.
      *set reputation %- 5
    *return
