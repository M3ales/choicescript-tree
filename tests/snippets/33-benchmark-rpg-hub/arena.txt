*temp fight_round 0
*temp opponent_hp 100
*temp player_hp 100
*temp fighting true

The arena roars with spectators. A scarred warrior faces you across the sand.

"Fight well, outsider."

*label combat_loop
*set fight_round + 1

Round ${fight_round}. Your HP: ${player_hp}. Opponent HP: ${opponent_hp}.

*if opponent_hp <= 0
  *goto victory
*if player_hp <= 0
  *goto defeat
*if fight_round > 10
  *goto draw

*choice
  #Attack with full force
    *temp damage 0
    *rand damage 15 30
    *set opponent_hp - damage
    You strike hard for ${damage} damage!
    *temp counter_damage 0
    *rand counter_damage 10 25
    *set player_hp - counter_damage
    The opponent counters for ${counter_damage}!
    *goto combat_loop
  #Defensive stance
    *temp damage 0
    *rand damage 5 15
    *set opponent_hp - damage
    You fight cautiously, dealing ${damage} damage.
    *temp counter_damage 0
    *rand counter_damage 5 15
    *set player_hp - counter_damage
    The opponent hits for ${counter_damage}, but your guard softens the blow.
    *goto combat_loop
  *selectable_if (charm >= 60) #Taunt the opponent
    *temp damage 0
    *rand damage 20 35
    *set opponent_hp - damage
    Your taunt enrages the opponent, creating an opening! ${damage} damage!
    *temp counter_damage 0
    *rand counter_damage 15 30
    *set player_hp - counter_damage
    But the fury cuts both ways. ${counter_damage} damage taken!
    *goto combat_loop

*label victory
*set arena_wins + 1
*set reputation %+ 20
*set gold + 25

The crowd erupts! You are victorious in ${fight_round} rounds!

*choice
  #Salute the crowd
    *set reputation %+ 10
    You raise your fist. The crowd loves it.
    *goto arena_exit
  #Help your opponent up
    *set reputation %+ 15
    *set charm + 5
    A show of honor. Even the opponent nods with respect.
    *goto arena_exit

*label defeat
*set reputation %- 10

You fall in round ${fight_round}. The crowd groans.

Better luck next time.
*goto arena_exit

*label draw
*set reputation %+ 5
*set gold + 10

After ${fight_round} rounds, the judge calls a draw. A respectable showing.
*goto arena_exit

*label arena_exit
You leave the arena sands behind.
*goto_scene startup village_hub
