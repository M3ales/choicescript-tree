*temp browsing true
*temp selected_item ""
*temp selected_cost 0

The shopkeeper greets you warmly. "Welcome! Browse my wares."

*label shop_hub

*if items_bought >= 3
  "You're one of my best customers!" the shopkeeper beams.

What catches your eye?

*choice
  *selectable_if (gold >= 30) #Sword of Valor (30 gold)
    *set selected_item "Sword of Valor"
    *set selected_cost 30
    *gosub confirm_purchase
    *if browsing
      *goto shop_hub
    *goto shop_exit
  *selectable_if (gold >= 20) #Shield of Faith (20 gold)
    *set selected_item "Shield of Faith"
    *set selected_cost 20
    *gosub confirm_purchase
    *if browsing
      *goto shop_hub
    *goto shop_exit
  *selectable_if (gold >= 15) #Charm Amulet (15 gold)
    *set selected_item "Charm Amulet"
    *set selected_cost 15
    *gosub confirm_purchase
    *if browsing
      *goto shop_hub
    *goto shop_exit
  *selectable_if (gold >= 10) #Health Potion (10 gold)
    *set selected_item "Health Potion"
    *set selected_cost 10
    *gosub confirm_purchase
    *if browsing
      *goto shop_hub
    *goto shop_exit
  #Leave the shop
    *goto shop_exit

*label shop_exit
"Come again!" calls the shopkeeper as you leave.
*goto_scene startup village_hub

*label confirm_purchase
You pick up the ${selected_item}. It costs ${selected_cost} gold. You have ${gold} gold.

*choice
  #Yes, buy it
    *set gold - selected_cost
    *set items_bought + 1
    *if selected_item = "Sword of Valor"
      *set strength + 15
    *if selected_item = "Shield of Faith"
      *set strength + 10
    *if selected_item = "Charm Amulet"
      *set charm + 15
    You bought the ${selected_item}!
    *goto confirm_continue
  #No, put it back
    You set the ${selected_item} back on the shelf.
    *goto confirm_continue

*label confirm_continue
*choice
  #Keep browsing
    *return
  #Leave the shop
    *set browsing false
    *return
