*create gold 100
*create reputation 50
*create strength 40
*create charm 60
*create quest_complete false
*create items_bought 0
*create arena_wins 0
*create tavern_visits 0
*create name ""

Welcome to the crossroads village.

*input_text name

Well met, ${name}. The village square stretches before you.

*label village_hub
*set tavern_visits + 1

You stand in the village square. Gold: ${gold}. Reputation: ${reputation}.

*choice
  *selectable_if (gold >= 10) #Visit the shop
    *goto_scene shop
  #Enter the tavern
    *goto_scene tavern
  *selectable_if (strength >= 30) #Challenge the arena
    *goto_scene arena
  #Check your stats
    *gosub show_stats
    *goto village_hub
  *selectable_if (quest_complete) #Leave the village
    *goto ending

*label ending
Your journey in the village ends.

You visited the tavern ${tavern_visits} times, won ${arena_wins} arena fights, and bought ${items_bought} items.

*if reputation >= 80
  The villagers cheer as you depart. A true hero!
  *goto final
*elseif reputation >= 50
  The villagers wave politely as you leave.
  *goto final
*else
  The villagers watch you leave with suspicious eyes.

*label final
Farewell, ${name}.
*ending "The End"

*label show_stats
Strength: ${strength}
Charm: ${charm}
Gold: ${gold}
Reputation: ${reputation}
Arena wins: ${arena_wins}
Items bought: ${items_bought}
*return
