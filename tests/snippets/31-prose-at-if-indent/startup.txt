*create val 0
*create marker ""
*create implicit_control_flow true

*label top
*temp local_bonus 0
*set local_bonus (val + 1)

=== TEST 1: val=100 (if matches) ===
*set val 100
*set marker ""
*if (val >= 100)
  IF-BODY
  *goto t1cont
*set marker (marker & "A")
Prose-between-if-and-elseif.
*elseif (val >= 50)
  ELSEIF-BODY
  *goto t1cont
*set marker (marker & "B")
Prose-between-elseif-and-else.
*else
  ELSE-BODY
  *goto t1cont
*set marker (marker & "C")
Prose-after-else.
*label t1cont
Marker1: ${marker}
